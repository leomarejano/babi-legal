Privacy Policy for Babi

Last updated: May 22, 2026

Babi ("we", "our", or "us") is an AI-powered grooming-style preview app for Shih Tzu owners. This policy explains what we collect, why, and how you can control it.

1. Information We Collect

  • Account information: email address and display name when you sign in with Google or email. We use Firebase Authentication.
  • Photos: images of your dog that you upload for AI styling. These are processed to generate your styled result and stored privately under your account in Firebase Storage.
  • Generated images: the styled results we create from your uploads, saved to your in-app FurGallery.
  • Subscription and purchase records: plan, billing period, expiry, and purchase tokens issued by Google Play. We do not see or store your payment-card details — those stay with Google Play.
  • Push notification tokens: a device-specific identifier issued by Firebase Cloud Messaging so we can send subscription renewal reminders and occasional re-engagement nudges. You can disable notifications anytime from system Settings.
  • Usage data: anonymous app interactions and screen views via Firebase Analytics, so we can understand which features are useful.
  • Crash and performance data: automatic crash reports and performance traces via Firebase Crashlytics, so we can fix bugs.
  • Advertising identifiers (Free tier only): the device's advertising ID is used by Google AdMob to serve rewarded ads.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To generate AI-styled previews of your dog and save them to your FurGallery
  • To process your subscription and grant the right number of monthly credits
  • To send transactional notifications (renewal reminders, occasional inactivity nudges) via Firebase Cloud Messaging
  • To serve rewarded ads to Free-tier users via Google AdMob
  • To diagnose crashes and improve app quality
  • To enforce abuse protections (quota limits, duplicate-subscription detection)

3. Third-Party Services

Babi relies on the following third-party services. Each has its own privacy policy:

  • Firebase (Google) — Authentication, Firestore, Storage, Cloud Functions, Cloud Messaging, Analytics, Crashlytics, App Check. firebase.google.com/support/privacy
  • Google Play Billing — subscription purchases and renewals. policies.google.com/privacy
  • Google AdMob — rewarded ads for Free-tier users. policies.google.com/technologies/ads
  • WaveSpeed AI — performs the AI image generation. Your photos are transmitted to WaveSpeed to produce the styled result; WaveSpeed does not retain them after generation. wavespeed.ai/privacy

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell your data, and we do not share your personal data with third parties for their own marketing purposes. The third-party services listed above process data strictly on our behalf to deliver the app's functionality.

5. Data Security

  • All data is transmitted over HTTPS (TLS).
  • Photos and account data are stored in Firebase, which encrypts data at rest.
  • App Check (Play Integrity) blocks unauthorized clients from accessing our backend.
  • Your subscription quota is enforced server-side so it cannot be tampered with from the device.

6. Data Retention & Deletion

  • Account data, photos, generated images, and history are kept until you delete your account.
  • Push notification tokens are deleted automatically when you sign out or uninstall the app.
  • Purchase tokens are retained for up to 90 days to handle billing reconciliation, then automatically deleted.
  • Crash reports and analytics events are retained per Firebase's default retention windows (typically 14 to 60 days).

7. How to Delete Your Data

You can permanently delete your account and all associated data at any time:

  1. Open the Babi app
  2. Go to Settings → Delete account
  3. Confirm the deletion

This will permanently remove your Firebase Authentication account, all uploaded photos, all generated images, your subscription quota record, your FurGallery history, and any push notification tokens registered to your account. The action cannot be undone.

If you have an active paid subscription, deleting your account does not cancel the subscription with Google Play — you must cancel separately from the Google Play Store to stop being charged.

If you cannot access the in-app deletion option for any reason, email us at babisupport@gmail.com and we will delete your data within 30 days.

8. Children's Privacy

Babi is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe we have collected information from a child, contact us and we will delete it.

9. Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction (Philippines Data Privacy Act, EU GDPR, California CCPA, etc.), you may have rights to access, correct, or delete your personal data. You can exercise most of these rights directly through Settings in the app, or by emailing babisupport@gmail.com.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy occasionally. Material changes will be communicated via an in-app notice or push notification. The "Last updated" date at the top reflects the most recent revision.

11. Contact

Questions or concerns? Email us at babisupport@gmail.com.
