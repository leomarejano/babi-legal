Privacy Policy for Babi

Last updated: April 26, 2026

1. Information We Collect

  • Account information: email address and display name when you sign in
  • Photos: images you upload for AI styling, processed and stored to generate your styled results
  • Usage data: app interactions and crash reports to improve the app

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To generate AI-styled images of your dog
  • To save your styling history (Premium users)
  • To serve ads (Free users) via Google AdMob
  • To process your subscription payments

3. Third-Party Services

We use the following services which have their own privacy policies:

  • Firebase (Google) — authentication, storage, and database
  • Google AdMob — advertising for free users
  • Google Play Billing — subscription payments
  • Replicate / WaveSpeed — AI image generation (your photos are not stored by them beyond generation)

4. Data Retention

  • Your account data is kept until you delete your account
  • Styled images are stored in your history until you delete them
  • You can request deletion of all your data by contacting us

5. Children's Privacy

Babi is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children.
